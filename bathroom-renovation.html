Specialized Bathroom Services

Detailed Scope of Work

The following checklist keeps your bathroom remodeling project on schedule while protecting the existing layout and elevating comfort, performance, and style.

  • Demolition & Preparation: Remove outdated fixtures, tile, flooring, and cabinetry as required, manage debris disposal responsibly, and prepare walls, subfloors, and ceilings for new installations.
  • Framing & Structural Work: Adjust or build supplemental wall framing when layouts shift, and install blocking to support vanities, mirrors, shower glass, and accessories.
  • Plumbing Upgrades: Relocate or enhance water supply and drain lines, install new shower, tub, sink, faucet, toilet, and bath accessories, and pressure test connections to stay leak-free.
  • Electrical & Lighting Enhancements: Upgrade wiring to current code, integrate GFCI outlets for safety, and add recessed, vanity, and ventilation lighting for balanced illumination.
  • Tile & Flooring Installation: Waterproof shower and tub surrounds, set tile on walls, floors, and backsplashes, and finish with the specified material such as porcelain tile or luxury vinyl.
  • Drywall & Painting Finishes: Hang, tape, and finish drywall to a smooth surface before applying moisture-resistant primer and designer-selected paint colors.
  • Cabinetry & Fixture Placement: Install vanity, medicine cabinet, or shelving units, mount mirrors, towel bars, and hardware, and properly seal countertops and sinks.
  • Detailing & Protection: Caulk and seal every wet area, align trim, and deliver polished finishing touches for long-term durability.
  • Final Walkthrough & Cleanup: Complete a meticulous clean, verify functionality across all systems, and hand over a move-in-ready bathroom.

Each phase is handled by seasoned specialists so your refreshed suite is ready for everyday use with confident craftsmanship and lasting comfort.
