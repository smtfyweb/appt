How do you manage moisture and mold risks in New England basements?  
Prior to framing, we inspect foundation walls and concrete slabs for hydrostatic pressure and moisture transmission. We install vapor barriers, rigid foam insulation against concrete walls, and moisture-resistant subfloor panels to ensure a dry, warm, and mold-free environment.

Do finished basements in Massachusetts require an egress window or door?  
If your finished basement design includes a bedroom, Massachusetts building code requires a code-compliant egress window or walk-out door for emergency escape. We help assess your layout and coordinate egress requirements.

Can you add a full or half bathroom to a finished basement?  
Yes. If underground plumbing rough-ins do not already exist, our licensed plumbing partners saw-cut the concrete slab to tie into the main sewer line, or install high-reliability sewage ejector pump systems.

What flooring options work best in a finished basement?  
Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) and glazed porcelain tile are the most durable and water-resistant choices for Massachusetts basements. They provide realistic wood aesthetics while remaining 100% waterproof.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our renovation process, permits, pricing, and warranties.

What is the difference between painting cabinets and cabinet refinishing?  
Regular house painting often relies on brush or roller application, which can leave brush strokes and chip easily. Our cabinet refinishing uses industrial spray systems with high-solids polyurethane/enamel coatings that create an ultra-durable, factory-smooth finish identical to brand-new high-end cabinets.

How much money does cabinet refinishing save compared to replacing cabinets?  
Refinishing existing solid wood cabinet boxes and doors typically saves 60% to 75% compared to custom cabinet replacement, while avoiding weeks of kitchen downtime and countertop removal.

How long does cabinet spray refinishing last?  
With proper care, our multi-coat catalyzed urethane finishes last 10 to 15+ years. They resist cooking grease, moisture, fingerprints, and household cleaning chemicals without chipping or peeling.

Do you spray cabinet doors on-site in my home?  
No. We carefully remove all cabinet doors and drawer fronts, label each piece, and transport them to our controlled spray facility for degreasing, sanding, priming, and spray finishing. Only the exterior cabinet frame boxes are prepped and sprayed in your home inside a fully sealed plastic spray containment booth.
