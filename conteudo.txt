Kitchen Renovation – Scope of Work

Our kitchen renovation services are designed to transform your space into a functional, modern, and stylish area that fits your lifestyle. We manage every step of the process with attention to detail and quality craftsmanship.

Scope of Work Includes:
	1.	Demolition & Preparation
	•	Remove existing cabinets, countertops, flooring, and fixtures as needed.
	•	Dispose of all debris in a safe and clean manner.
	•	Prepare subfloors, walls, and ceilings for new finishes.
	2.	Framing & Structural Adjustments
	•	Modify or build new wall framing if layout changes are required.
	•	Install blocking for cabinetry, islands, or shelving.
	3.	Electrical & Lighting
	•	Upgrade wiring to meet current code.
	•	Install new outlets, switches, and dedicated circuits for appliances.
	•	Add recessed lighting, under-cabinet lights, or decorative fixtures.
	4.	Plumbing
	•	Relocate or upgrade water and drain lines as needed.
	•	Install new sink, faucet, and dishwasher connections.
	•	Ensure all plumbing is tested and ready for use.
	5.	Cabinetry & Storage
	•	Install custom or prefabricated cabinets.
	•	Add soft-close hinges, pull-out organizers, and specialty storage options.
	•	Finish with trim, crown molding, and hardware.
	6.	Countertops & Backsplash
	•	Template, fabricate, and install countertops (granite, quartz, butcher block, etc.).
	•	Install backsplash (tile, stone, or glass) with detailed finishing.
	7.	Flooring
	•	Prepare subfloor for proper installation.
	•	Install tile, hardwood, or luxury vinyl flooring.
	•	Seal and finish for durability.
	8.	Painting & Finishing
	•	Repair, patch, and smooth wall and ceiling surfaces.
	•	Apply primer and two finish coats of premium paint.
	•	Final detailing for a clean, polished appearance.
	9.	Final Walkthrough & Cleanup
	•	Thoroughly clean the work area.
	•	Inspect all installations for quality and function.
	•	Ensure your kitchen is move-in ready.


Bathroom Renovation – Scope of Work

A bathroom should be both practical and comfortable, with finishes that fit your style. Our bathroom renovation services cover every stage of the project to ensure a smooth process and high-quality results.

Scope of Work Includes:
	1.	Demolition & Preparation
	•	Remove existing fixtures, tile, flooring, and cabinetry as required.
	•	Dispose of all debris properly.
	•	Prepare walls, subfloor, and ceiling for new installations.
	2.	Framing & Structural Work
	•	Adjust or build new wall framing if layout changes are needed.
	•	Install blocking for vanities, mirrors, and accessories.
	3.	Plumbing
	•	Relocate or upgrade water supply and drain lines.
	•	Install new shower, tub, sink, faucet, toilet, and bath accessories.
	•	Ensure all plumbing connections are tested and leak-free.
	4.	Electrical & Lighting
	•	Upgrade wiring to meet current code requirements.
	•	Install GFCI outlets for safety.
	•	Add recessed lighting, vanity lights, or exhaust fans.
	5.	Tile & Flooring
	•	Prepare and waterproof shower and tub areas.
	•	Install tile for shower walls, floors, and backsplash.
	•	Install flooring (tile, vinyl, or other specified material).
	6.	Drywall & Painting
	•	Hang, tape, and finish drywall for smooth surfaces.
	•	Apply moisture-resistant primer and two coats of paint.
	7.	Cabinetry & Fixtures
	•	Install vanity, medicine cabinet, or shelving units.
	•	Mount mirrors, towel bars, and other hardware.
	•	Fit countertops and sinks with proper sealing.
	8.	Finishing Touches
	•	Caulk and seal all wet areas for long-lasting protection.
	•	Final detailing to ensure a polished, finished look.
	9.	Final Walkthrough & Cleanup
	•	Thoroughly clean and prepare the bathroom for use.
	•	Inspect all work to guarantee functionality and quality.
	•	Deliver a complete, move-in-ready bathroom.

Basement Renovation – Scope of Work

Transforming an unfinished basement into a finished living space adds comfort, functionality, and value to your home. We handle every step of the renovation process, from framing to final finishes, to create a space that meets your needs—whether it’s a family room, home office, gym, or guest suite.

Scope of Work Includes:
	1.	Preparation & Demolition
	•	Clear out existing materials, debris, or old framing as needed.
	•	Inspect for moisture or structural issues before construction begins.
	•	Apply waterproofing solutions if required.
	2.	Framing & Layout
	•	Frame new walls, closets, or rooms according to the design.
	•	Build soffits to conceal ductwork, plumbing, or electrical runs.
	•	Frame openings for doors and windows.
	3.	Insulation & Drywall
	•	Install insulation for energy efficiency and soundproofing.
	•	Hang, tape, and finish drywall for smooth, paint-ready surfaces.
	4.	Electrical & Lighting
	•	Upgrade or install new electrical wiring to meet code.
	•	Add outlets, switches, and circuits for appliances or entertainment systems.
	•	Install recessed lighting, ceiling fixtures, or accent lighting.
	5.	Plumbing (if required)
	•	Rough-in and install plumbing for bathrooms, wet bars, or laundry rooms.
	•	Connect all fixtures and test for proper operation.
	6.	HVAC & Ventilation
	•	Extend heating and cooling ducts for comfort.
	•	Install vents or registers to maintain air circulation.
	7.	Flooring
	•	Prepare concrete slab or subfloor.
	•	Install flooring (carpet, vinyl, laminate, or engineered hardwood).
	8.	Ceilings & Trim
	•	Install drywall ceilings or drop ceilings as specified.
	•	Add baseboards, casing, and crown molding for a finished look.
	9.	Painting & Finishes
	•	Prime and paint all walls, ceilings, and trim.
	•	Apply two coats of premium paint for a clean, polished finish.
	10.	Final Walkthrough & Cleanup
	•	Thoroughly clean the entire space.
	•	Inspect all work for safety, quality, and function.
	•	Deliver a fully finished basement ready to use.
