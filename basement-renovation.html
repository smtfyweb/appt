Basement Renovation Services

Scope of Work

Our comprehensive basement remodeling scope covers every critical phase:

  • Preparation & Demolition: Clear out existing materials and debris, inspect for moisture or structural issues, and apply waterproofing solutions as needed.
  • Framing & Layout: Frame new walls, closets, and rooms according to your design. Build soffits to conceal ductwork, plumbing, or electrical runs. Frame openings for doors and windows.
  • Insulation & Drywall: Install insulation for energy efficiency and soundproofing. Hang, tape, and finish drywall for smooth, paint-ready surfaces.
  • Electrical & Lighting: Upgrade or install wiring, outlets, switches, and circuits. Install recessed, ceiling, or accent lighting to fit your design.
  • Plumbing (if required): Rough-in and install plumbing for bathrooms, wet bars, or laundry rooms. Connect all fixtures and ensure proper operation.
  • HVAC & Ventilation: Extend heating and cooling ducts for comfort. Install vents or registers to maintain air circulation.
  • Flooring: Prepare subfloor or concrete slab. Install carpet, vinyl, laminate, or engineered hardwood flooring.
  • Ceilings & Trim: Install drywall or drop ceilings as specified. Add baseboards, casing, and crown molding for a polished finish.
  • Painting & Finishes: Prime and paint walls, ceilings, and trim. Apply two coats of premium paint for a clean, professional look.
  • Final Walkthrough & Cleanup: Thoroughly clean the space, inspect all work for quality, and deliver a fully finished basement ready to use.
