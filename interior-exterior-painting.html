Our Methodology for Exterior Painting

Transparent Estimation

When engaging Opportunity Painting for your Exterior House Painting needs, you'll collaborate directly with the owner, who will furnish a candid estimate encompassing the entire project with no concealed charges.

  • The owner conducts an on-site assessment to gather project specifics
  • A complimentary, no-obligation quote is provided
  • Exterior painting is promptly scheduled
  • A project timeline is communicated to you
  • You select paint hues

Given the uniqueness of each painting project, an accurate estimate can only be determined after an assessment of your home's condition and the scope of necessary work. This personalized approach ensures precise and comprehensive estimates for our clients.

Comprehensive Preparation

Recognizing the pivotal role of preparation in achieving impeccable paint finishes, the Opportunity Painting team adheres to meticulous steps to prime all surfaces before commencing painting.

  • Thorough surface washing to eliminate mildew or mold
  • Elimination of loose paint through sanding
  • Preparation of glossy areas through scuffing and sanding
  • Gaps are filled with caulk to prevent water infiltration

Every aspect of the preparatory phase for your exterior painting project is conducted meticulously, with utmost attention to detail and care for your property. We maintain constant communication throughout the project to ensure your comfort and satisfaction.

Proficient Painting and Methodical Cleanup

Following thorough surface preparation, the exterior painting process commences. Our adept team at Opportunity Painting primes all painted surfaces to ensure uniformity and adheres to specific steps:

  • Application of primer for consistent finish
  • Use of premium-quality paints for durability
  • Regular cleanup of tools and materials to maintain cleanliness
  • Post-painting inspection with you to ensure complete satisfaction

Our exterior painting services are tailored to minimize disruptions to your daily routine. Unable to be present during painting? Not an issue. We accommodate your schedule to ensure minimal interference.

In addition to exterior painting, we also provide various other services to meet your needs. Explore our interior painting, commercial painting, light carpentry, and kitchen cabinet refinishing services for comprehensive property enhancement.
