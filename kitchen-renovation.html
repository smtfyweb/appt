Specialized Kitchen Services

Detailed Scope of Work

The following checklist keeps your kitchen remodeling project on schedule while protecting surrounding rooms and delivering a cohesive, chef-ready workspace.

  • Demolition & Site Prep: Remove outdated cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring, and soffits as required, manage debris responsibly, and shield adjacent rooms with dust barriers.
  • Structural & Layout Adjustments: Modify walls, build pony walls, and install blocking to support islands, ceiling treatments, open shelving, and specialty storage.
  • Plumbing & Gas Rough-Ins: Relocate supply, drain, and gas lines, set valves for sinks and appliances, and pressure test every connection before close-up.
  • Electrical & Lighting: Upgrade wiring to current code, add dedicated appliance circuits, and stage layered lighting including recessed, under-cabinet, and decorative fixtures.
  • Ventilation & HVAC: Configure ductwork for range hoods, integrate make-up air when required, and coordinate with existing HVAC for balanced airflow.
  • Cabinetry & Storage Systems: Install base, wall, and tall cabinets with precision leveling, mount organizers, and verify soft-close hardware performs smoothly.
  • Countertops & Surfaces: Template, fabricate, and set stone, quartz, or butcher block tops, seam and seal surfaces, and install full-height backsplash materials.
  • Flooring Installation: Prepare subfloors, set tile, hardwood, or luxury vinyl plank, and finish transitions for a durable, easy-to-clean foundation.
  • Finishing Touches & Handoff: Install trim, paint, mount fixtures and appliances, complete punch-list touch-ups, and deliver a spotless, move-in-ready kitchen.

Each phase is handled by seasoned specialists so your refreshed kitchen is ready for everyday use with confident craftsmanship and lasting performance.
