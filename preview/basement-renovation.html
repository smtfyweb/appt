COMPREHENSIVE SCOPE OF WORK

Our 10-Step Basement Finishing Process

Engineered for New England homes: moisture barriers, thermal insulation, and building code compliance.

01

Moisture Assessment & Prep

Inspecting concrete foundation walls and slabs for dampness, applying vapor barriers before framing.

02

Framing & Soffits

Perimeter wall framing with thermal breaks, dividing rooms, and framing custom soffits around pipes.

03

Insulation & Soundproofing

Rigid foam wall insulation and mineral wool acoustic batts in ceiling joists for quiet comfort.

04

Electrical & Recessed Lights

Wiring code-compliant circuits, ultra-slim LED recessed ceiling lighting, AV runs, and smoke/CO detectors.

05

Plumbing (Wet Bars / Baths)

Connecting to sewer stacks or installing sewage ejector systems for basement bathrooms and laundry rooms.

06

HVAC Duct Extensions

Extending heating and cooling branch ducts for balanced year-round climate control and air circulation.

07

Waterproof Flooring

Subfloor vapor membranes followed by 100% waterproof luxury vinyl plank (LVP) or ceramic tile.

08

Ceilings & Trim

Seamless drywall ceilings or acoustic drop panels, plus wide baseboards and custom door casings.

09

Drywall & Finish Painting

Level 4 drywall taping, primer, and two coats of washable Benjamin Moore paint on all surfaces.

10

Final Clean & Handover

Full cleanup, fixture commissioning, and comprehensive client walkthrough ready for immediate use.
