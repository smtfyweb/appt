COMPREHENSIVE SCOPE OF WORK

Our 9-Step Kitchen Renovation Process

Proven craftsmanship ensuring timeline transparency, building code compliance, and turnkey quality.

01

Demolition & Site Prep

Careful removal of old cabinets, countertops, flooring, and fixtures. Dust containment barriers throughout.

02

Framing & Structural

Modifying walls, building pony walls, and installing solid blocking for islands, pantries, and wall-mounted cabinetry.

03

Electrical & Lighting

Upgrading wiring to MA code. Dedicated circuits, GFCI outlets, recessed slim ceiling lights, and under-cabinet LED lighting.

04

Plumbing Relocation

Relocating water supply lines and drain stacks for sinks, dishwashers, pot-fillers, and ice makers. Leak tested.

05

Precision Cabinetry

Laser-level setting of custom cabinets. Specialty pantry pullouts, soft-close hardware, and custom crown molding.

06

Countertops & Backsplash

Digital templating and fabrication of quartz or granite tops. Expert tile backsplash installation with epoxy grout.

07

Flooring Installation

Subfloor leveling and moisture testing. Setting luxury vinyl plank, hardwood, or porcelain floor tile with seamless transitions.

08

Painting & Finish Trim

Drywall skim coating, primer, and two finish coats of Benjamin Moore paint. Baseboards, window casing, and hardware detailing.

09

Final Walkthrough

Deep cleaning of the kitchen, appliance commissioning, and comprehensive client punch-list inspection.
