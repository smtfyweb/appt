COMPREHENSIVE SCOPE OF WORK

Our 9-Step Bathroom Renovation Process

Tested methodology ensuring 100% leak-proof waterproofing and showroom-grade craftsmanship.

01

Demolition & Removal

Tearing out old tubs, showers, tiles, vanities, and subflooring with zero damage to surrounding walls.

02

Framing & Solid Blocking

Squaring studs for straight tile walls, adding backing for grab bars, floating vanities, and heavy glass doors.

03

Plumbing Relocation

Rough-in of supply and waste lines. Installing thermostatic valves, handheld sprays, and toilet flanges.

04

Electrical, GFCI & Venting

GFCI circuits, LED vanity lighting, shower ceiling lights, and quiet exterior-vented exhaust fans.

05

Schluter Waterproofing & Tile

Installing waterproof membranes and pre-sloped pans. Setting porcelain, ceramic, or marble tile with epoxy grout.

06

Moisture-Resistant Drywall

Green board drywall installation, mold-resistant skim coating, and Benjamin Moore Aura Bath & Spa finish paint.

07

Vanity, Countertops & Sinks

Setting single or double sink vanities, quartz tops, undermount sinks, faucets, mirrors, and towel bars.

08

Glass Enclosures & Sealants

Installing custom frameless glass shower doors and 100% silicone mold-resistant perimeter caulking.

09

Testing & Final Walkthrough

Water-testing all fixtures, detailed cleanup, and delivering a spotless, move-in-ready bathroom.
