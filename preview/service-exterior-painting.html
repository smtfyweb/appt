WEATHER PROTECTION

A Methodical Approach for Long-Lasting Protection

Painting a New England exterior is serious business. Without rigorous surface preparation, even the best paint will blister and peel within two seasons.

At Opportunity Painting & Renovation Services Inc., owner Juliney 'Ney' Santana oversees the preparation process directly. We ensure surfaces are dry, scraped down to sound wood, sealed with premium oil or bonding primers, and coated with flexible acrylic latex paints that expand and contract with seasonal temperature swings.

  • ✓ High-Pressure Wash: Eradicating mildew, grime, and oxidation.
  • ✓ Scraping & Feather Sanding: Removing loose flaking paint.
  • ✓ Exterior Wood Rot Replacement: Replacing rotted trim with PVC or cedar.
  • ✓ Premium Caulking: Sealing all joints around windows and doors to block water intrusion.
