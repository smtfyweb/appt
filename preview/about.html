About Opportunity Painting Inc.

Over 15 years of trusted residential painting and complete home renovations in Holliston & MetroWest MA.

OUR COMMITMENT

Craftsmanship You Can See. Reliability You Can Trust.

Founded by Juliney "Ney" Santana, Opportunity Painting & Renovation Services Inc. was built on a simple yet unwavering conviction: that home improvement contractors should deliver both flawless physical artistry and transparent, courteous communication.

Based out of 7 Franklin St, Holliston, MA, we have spent over 15 years serving homeowners throughout Middlesex, Norfolk, and Worcester counties. From historic New England colonial restorations to sleek, contemporary kitchen renovations, we bring seasoned expertise to every phase of construction.
